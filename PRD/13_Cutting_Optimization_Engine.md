# 1. Overview

The Cutting Optimization Engine is responsible for optimizing generated production boards into efficient cutting layouts for manufacturing.

Its responsibility is to maximize material utilization while preserving all engineering results generated by the Production Formula Engine.

The Cutting Optimization Engine does not calculate board dimensions.

It does not create Engineering Rules.

It does not modify Furniture Objects.

It does not modify Production Formula results.

Its responsibility is only to optimize cutting layouts using confirmed production boards.

The Cutting Optimization Engine supports efficient factory production by reducing material waste and improving sheet utilization.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall:

- Optimize cutting layouts.
- Maximize material utilization.
- Maximize remnant utilization.
- Minimize material waste.
- Generate optimized cutting layouts.
- Generate cutting production documents.
- Generate purchasing requirements.
- Support production review.

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall optimize confirmed production boards only.

It shall never modify engineering calculations.

# 4. Optimization Principles

The Cutting Optimization Engine performs layout optimization only.

Every optimization shall preserve:

- Board Dimensions
- Material Specifications
- Grain Direction
- Edge Banding Requirements
- Processing Information

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall never:

- Modify Board Dimensions
- Modify Engineering Rules
- Modify Cabinet Structures
- Modify Production Formula Results

The final cutting layout shall always represent the confirmed production information.

---

# 5. Material Group Optimization

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall group production boards before optimization.

Grouping criteria may include:

- Material
- Thickness
- Surface Finish
- Laminate
- Grain Direction
- Edge Banding Requirements

Each Material Group shall be optimized independently.

Production boards from different Material Groups shall never be mixed during optimization.

---

# 6. Whole Project Optimization

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall optimize the entire project before performing any optional partial optimization.

The optimization scope shall include every confirmed production board within the selected project.

The objective is to maximize overall material utilization across the entire project rather than optimizing individual cabinets independently.

Whole Project Optimization shall be the default optimization mode.

---

# 7. Mother Sheet Optimization

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall optimize production boards using commercially available Mother Sheets.

Mother Sheet specifications shall originate from the Official Engineering Knowledge Base.

Supported Mother Sheet information may include:

- Sheet Dimensions
- Material
- Thickness
- Surface Finish
- Grain Direction

Only valid Mother Sheet specifications shall be used during optimization.

---

# 8. Remnant Management

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall automatically manage all usable Remnants generated during cutting.

Every Remnant shall contain:

- Remnant ID
- Source Mother Sheet
- Material
- Thickness
- Dimensions
- Remaining Area
- Availability Status

Example:

Sheet05-R01

Material:
18mm White CB

Size:
748 × 2400

Every usable Remnant shall become part of the current Project Remnant Library.

---

# 9. Remnant Priority

Before allocating a new Mother Sheet, the Cutting Optimization Engine shall search the Project Remnant Library.

If a suitable Remnant is available, the system shall prioritize using the Remnant.

Only when no suitable Remnant satisfies the production requirements may a new Mother Sheet be allocated.

The objective is to maximize material utilization while minimizing waste throughout the entire project.

---

# 10. Purchase Requirements

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall automatically generate Purchase Requirements after optimization has been completed.

Purchase Requirements shall be generated using optimized Mother Sheet allocation.

Purchase information may include:

- Material
- Thickness
- Surface Finish
- Mother Sheet Size
- Required Quantity

Purchase Requirements shall always represent calculated optimization results.

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall never automatically increase purchasing quantities.

Final purchasing decisions shall always belong to the user.

---

# 11. Whole Project Cutting Summary

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall automatically generate a Whole Project Cutting Summary.

The summary shall:

- Group by Material
- Group by Thickness
- Group by Surface Finish
- Group identical Board Dimensions
- Combine total quantities
- Sort Board Dimensions from largest to smallest

The Whole Project Cutting Summary provides Cutting Operators with a simplified production reference.

Every summary shall originate from confirmed Production Formula results.

---

# 12. Cabinet Cutting Summary

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall automatically generate a Cutting Summary for every confirmed Furniture Object.

Each Cabinet Cutting Summary may include:

- Cabinet Name
- Cabinet Number
- Material Group
- Board Dimensions
- Board Quantity
- Board Description
- Processing Information
- Manufacturing Requirements

The Cabinet Cutting Summary is intended for factory production personnel.

Every Cabinet Cutting Summary shall originate from confirmed Furniture Objects and Production Formula results.

---

# 13. Cutting Layout

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall automatically generate a Cutting Layout for every allocated Mother Sheet.

Every Cutting Layout shall display:

- Mother Sheet ID
- Material
- Board Arrangement
- Board ID
- Board Dimensions
- Part Number
- Furniture Object Reference
- Remaining Material
- Remnant ID (if applicable)

Each allocated Mother Sheet shall have one Cutting Layout.

Every Cutting Layout shall accurately represent the optimized cutting arrangement.

---

# 14. Remnant Reference

Whenever a Production Board is cut from an existing Remnant, the Cutting Optimization Engine shall record the Remnant Source.

Every Remnant Reference may include:

- Source Mother Sheet
- Remnant ID
- Material
- Thickness
- Original Sheet Reference

Example:

Board ID

LP018

↓

Source

Sheet03-R01

The Cutting Layout shall clearly indicate whether each Production Board originates from a new Mother Sheet or an existing Remnant.

This allows Cutting Operators to locate materials efficiently during production.

---

# 15. Batch Optimization

After Whole Project Optimization has been completed, the Cutting Optimization Engine may support optional Batch Optimization.

Batch Optimization allows users to optimize production for selected scopes, such as:

- Selected Floor
- Selected Room
- Selected Cabinet Group
- Selected Furniture Objects

Before Batch Optimization is confirmed, the Cutting Optimization Engine shall automatically compare:

- Additional Mother Sheets Required
- Additional Material Usage
- Additional Material Waste
- Remnant Utilization

The final optimization decision shall always belong to the user.

---

# 16. Material Utilization Report

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall automatically generate a Material Utilization Report.

The report may include:

- Material Utilization (%)
- Material Waste (%)
- Remnant Utilization (%)
- Total Mother Sheets Used
- Total Remnants Generated
- Total Remnants Reused
- Estimated Material Savings

The Material Utilization Report is intended for production analysis only.

The report shall never determine purchasing or production decisions automatically.

---

# 17. Optimization Reliability

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall optimize confirmed Production Boards only.

The engine shall never:

- Modify Board Dimensions
- Modify Furniture Objects
- Modify Engineering Rules
- Modify Production Formula Results
- Mix Different Material Groups
- Ignore Grain Direction
- Ignore Material Thickness
- Ignore Processing Requirements

If optimization cannot be completed successfully, the system shall generate a Production Issue and request user confirmation.

The Cutting Optimization Engine may provide optimization recommendations but shall never make production decisions automatically.

---

# 18. Design Principles

The Cutting Optimization Engine shall follow these principles:

1. Production Formula Driven
2. Furniture Object Driven
3. Whole Project Optimization First
4. Maximum Material Utilization
5. Maximum Remnant Utilization
6. Automatic Cutting Layout Generation
7. No Independent Engineering Calculations
8. No Modification of Production Boards
9. Fully Offline Operation
10. Expandable Through Official Engineering Knowledge

---

# 19. Scope

This PRD defines only the Cutting Optimization Engine.

The following systems are defined separately:

- AI Recognition Engine
- Official Engineering Knowledge Base
- Factory Reference Database System
- Furniture Object Engine
- Production Formula Engine
- Manufacturing Requirements Engine
- Furniture Shop Drawing Generation Engine
- Project Document Package Engine
- Purchase Generation System

The Cutting Optimization Engine is responsible only for optimizing confirmed Production Boards into efficient cutting layouts and generating cutting-related production documents.

Engineering calculations belong exclusively to the Production Formula Engine.

Engineering knowledge belongs exclusively to the Official Engineering Knowledge Base.

Purchasing decisions belong exclusively to the user.
