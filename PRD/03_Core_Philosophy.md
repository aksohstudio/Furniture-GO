# 1. Philosophy

Furniture GO is built around the real engineering and production workflow used by furniture professionals.

The system does not begin with calculation.

It begins with understanding.

Furniture GO understands the project before generating engineering information or production data.

---

# 2. Understand Before Calculate

Traditional software focuses on calculation.

Furniture GO focuses on understanding.

Before calculation begins,

Furniture GO understands:

- The Project
- The Environment
- The Rooms
- The Furniture
- The Engineering Knowledge
- The Production Requirements

Only after sufficient understanding does automatic production calculation begin.

---

# 3. Recognize Before Generate

Furniture GO never generates production documents immediately.

The system first performs:

- Project Recognition
- Furniture Recognition
- Engineering Review
- Project Confirmation

Only after every important issue has been confirmed will production documents be generated.

---

# 4. Knowledge Before Automation

Automation alone is not valuable.

Correct automation is valuable.

Furniture GO is built upon engineering knowledge collected from experienced furniture professionals.

The Official Engineering Knowledge Base preserves:

- Engineering Standards
- Construction Methods
- Material Knowledge
- Hardware Knowledge
- Processing Methods
- Manufacturing Experience

Knowledge always comes before automation.

---

# 5. Assist, Not Replace

Furniture GO does not replace furniture professionals.

Furniture GO assists them.

The system provides:

- Recognition
- Analysis
- Suggestions
- Comparisons
- Automatic Calculations

The final engineering and production decision always belongs to the user.

---

# 6. Engineering First

Furniture GO follows standardized engineering knowledge before production calculation begins.

Engineering knowledge defines:

- Construction Logic
- Material Rules
- Hardware Rules
- Manufacturing Methods
- Production Formulas

Factory Configuration provides default preferences only.

Project-specific decisions always belong to the user.

---

# 7. Project First

Furniture GO treats every project as one complete engineering and production system.

The system understands the complete project before analyzing individual furniture.

Furniture GO studies:

Project

↓

Room

↓

Furniture

↓

Engineering Information

↓

Production Information

Every engineering decision belongs to the corresponding project.

---

# 8. Intelligence Before Features

Furniture GO is not built by adding more features.

Its value comes from engineering intelligence.

Features such as:

- Material List
- Hardware List
- Cutting List
- Manufacturing Requirements
- Shop Drawings
- Quotation
- Project Package

are the results of intelligent engineering analysis,

not independent functions.

---

# 9. Preserve Knowledge

Engineering knowledge should never disappear when experienced professionals retire or leave.

Furniture GO transforms years of engineering experience into reusable digital knowledge.

The Official Engineering Knowledge Base continuously preserves engineering knowledge for future generations.

Knowledge becomes a reusable engineering asset rather than personal experience.

---

# 10. Separate Responsibilities

Furniture GO separates the complete workflow into independent systems.

Recognition,

Engineering,

and Production

are independent responsibilities.

This separation ensures that:

- Recognition remains objective.
- Engineering remains reliable.
- Production remains consistent.
- Every calculation is traceable.
- Every production result originates from confirmed engineering information.

---

# 11. Core Principles

Furniture GO understands before it calculates.

Furniture GO recognizes before it generates.

Furniture GO reviews before it confirms.

Furniture GO confirms before it produces.

Furniture GO assists before it automates.

Users always make the final engineering and production decisions.

---

# 12. Product Belief

The greatest asset of Furniture GO is not its software.

The greatest asset is its engineering knowledge.

Technology will continue to evolve.

Artificial Intelligence will continue to improve.

However,

engineering knowledge,

manufacturing experience,

and furniture production logic

remain the foundation of the entire Furniture GO ecosystem.
