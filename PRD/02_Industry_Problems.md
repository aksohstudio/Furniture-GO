# 1. Industry Overview

The custom furniture industry still relies heavily on manual work.

Although designers produce professional drawings,

most production preparation still depends on experienced furniture professionals.

The knowledge required for production is rarely standardized or digitized.

---

# 2. Information Gap

Furniture production passes through multiple stages.

```text
Designer

↓

Project Drawings

↓

Furniture Professional

↓

Factory Production

↓

Installation
```

The largest gap exists between designer drawings and factory production.

Designer drawings describe design intent.

Factories require production information.

Most of this conversion is completed manually by experienced furniture professionals.

---

# 3. Knowledge Dependency

Many production decisions are based on personal experience rather than documented rules.

Examples include:

- Cabinet Splitting
- Board Construction
- Closing Width
- Visible Panels
- Surface Finish
- Joint Position
- Hardware Selection
- Material Selection

Different factories often produce different results from the same designer drawing.

---

# 4. Manual Production Preparation

Before production begins,

furniture professionals must manually:

- Study the entire project
- Identify furniture-related drawings
- Compare designer drawings with factory standards
- Visit the site
- Measure actual dimensions
- Confirm construction details
- Calculate board sizes
- Prepare cutting lists
- Calculate hardware
- Prepare quotations

These tasks consume significant time and rely heavily on individual experience.

---

# 5. Lack of Standardization

Most production knowledge exists only inside experienced workers.

Examples include:

- Factory Rules
- Construction Methods
- Cabinet Logic
- Material Rules
- Hardware Rules
- Installation Experience

When experienced workers leave,

valuable production knowledge is often lost.

---

# 6. Production Risks

Manual production preparation increases the risk of:

- Incorrect dimensions
- Missing information
- Material waste
- Hardware mistakes
- Quotation errors
- Construction conflicts
- Production delays

Most mistakes are discovered only after production has already started.

---

# 7. Existing Software Limitations

Many existing software solutions focus on:

- CAD Drawing
- 3D Design
- CNC Manufacturing
- Material Calculation

However,

they generally assume that production information has already been confirmed.

The real production preparation process—

including project understanding,

environment recognition,

site verification,

factory rule comparison,

and production review—

still depends largely on experienced professionals.

---

# 8. Industry Needs

The furniture industry requires a system that can:

- Understand the entire project
- Identify furniture-related information
- Analyze construction environments
- Compare designer specifications with factory rules
- Detect production conflicts
- Preserve production knowledge
- Assist professionals in making production decisions
- Generate production documents automatically

---

# 9. Furniture GO Solution

Furniture GO bridges the gap between designer drawings and factory production.

Instead of replacing experienced professionals,

Furniture GO captures their production knowledge,

assists their decision-making,

and automates repetitive production preparation.

The system transforms traditional production experience into reusable digital intelligence.

---

# 10. Core Philosophy

Furniture GO is not designed to replace people.

Furniture GO is designed to preserve knowledge.

It understands projects.

It understands production.

It assists professionals.

Users always make the final production decision.
