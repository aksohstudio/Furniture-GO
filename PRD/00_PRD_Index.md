# 1. Purpose

The Product Requirement Document (PRD) serves as the single source of truth for the Furniture GO project.

Its purpose is to organize the complete product vision, engineering knowledge, production workflow, system architecture, development principles, and business rules into a long-term maintainable standard.

All product decisions, feature planning, system architecture, and future development shall follow the official PRD.

---

# 2. PRD-First Development

Furniture GO follows a PRD-First development methodology.

The complete product architecture shall be confirmed before implementation begins.

Objectives include:

- Reduce unnecessary code changes.
- Define complete system responsibilities before development.
- Maintain a consistent product direction.
- Preserve long-term maintainability.
- Allow future developers to continue development using the same architecture.

---

# 3. Development Workflow

Furniture GO follows the workflow below.

```text
Requirement

↓

Discussion

↓

Architecture Review

↓

PRD Update

↓

Product Owner Approval

↓

Development

↓

Testing

↓

Completion
```

No feature may enter development before it has been documented and approved within the official PRD.

---

# 4. PRD Rules

Every product requirement shall follow the same process.

```text
Proposal

↓

Discussion

↓

Architecture Review

↓

Product Owner Approval

↓

Document in PRD

↓

Development
```

Rules:

- Unconfirmed ideas shall never become part of the official PRD.
- Every PRD shall define one primary responsibility.
- Responsibilities shall never overlap between different PRDs.
- PRDs shall remain modular and independent.
- The PRD is always the primary reference for Furniture GO development.

---

# 5. Product Owner

The Product Owner is responsible for:

- Defining product direction.
- Providing furniture industry knowledge.
- Providing engineering workflow.
- Approving all product requirements.
- Making final product decisions.
- Approving all official PRD documents.

# 9. Core Principles

Furniture GO follows these principles.

- Understand before Calculate
- Analyze before Generate
- Confirm before Production
- Engineering before Manufacturing
- One Responsibility per System
- One Source of Truth per Data Type
- Database-Driven Architecture
- Modular Architecture
- Fully Offline Operation
- Long-term Maintainability

---

# 10. PRD Maintenance

All official PRDs shall be version controlled.

Whenever a PRD changes:

- Version Number shall be updated.
- Revision History shall be recorded.
- Product Owner approval shall be required.
- Related PRDs shall remain consistent.

Historical PRDs shall be preserved for future reference.

Official PRDs shall never be overwritten without version tracking.
