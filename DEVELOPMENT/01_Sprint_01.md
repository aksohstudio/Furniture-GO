# 1. Purpose

Sprint 01 establishes the technical foundation of Furniture GO Version 1.

This Sprint focuses entirely on project initialization and development infrastructure.

No production features or business logic shall be implemented during this Sprint.

The objective is to prepare a stable, maintainable and scalable project for future development.

---

# 2. Sprint Goal

Complete the development foundation required for all future Furniture GO modules.

The completed foundation shall support:

- PRD Architecture
- UI Architecture
- Modular Development
- Offline Operation
- Future Expansion

# 5. Folder Structure

The project shall adopt a modular folder structure.

Example

```text
/src

/components

/pages

/layouts

/modules

/services

/database

/router

/hooks

/utils

/constants

/styles

/assets

/types
```

The structure shall remain scalable throughout Version 1.
