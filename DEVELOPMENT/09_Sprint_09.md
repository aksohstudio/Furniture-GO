# 1. Purpose

Sprint 09 develops the Purchase List Workspace.

The Purchase List Workspace automatically converts approved Furniture Objects into purchasing information required for production.

The workspace is responsible for:

- Purchase List Generation
- Material Purchasing
- Hardware Purchasing
- Purchase Summary
- Purchasing Reports

The Purchase List Workspace prepares purchasing information only.

It does not manage suppliers, inventory, ERP or purchasing workflows.

---

# 2. Sprint Goal

Complete the Purchase List Workspace required by Version 1.

The completed workspace shall support:

- Purchase List Generation
- Material Purchasing
- Hardware Purchasing

- Export Functions
